The Cone Sleeves is a leading manufacturer of high-quality paper products, specializing in custom cone sleeves for ice cream, waffles, and others like sugar cones. With a passion for innovation and a commitment to exceptional customer service, we strive to exceed our client’s expectations and provide them with the best possible solutions for their packaging needs.

Our state-of-the-art facility is equipped with the latest technology and machinery, enabling us to produce cone sleeves that are attractive, functional, and environmentally friendly. We use only the highest quality materials and employ rigorous quality control measures to ensure our products meet our exacting standards.

At The Cone Sleeves, we understand the importance of branding and the role that packaging plays in establishing and reinforcing brand identity. That’s why we work closely with our clients to create custom designs that reflect their unique vision and communicate their brand message effectively.

We pride ourselves in delivering quality products, exceptional customer service, and competitive pricing. Whether you’re a small artisanal ice cream shop or a large franchise chain, we have the expertise and experience to meet your packaging needs.
