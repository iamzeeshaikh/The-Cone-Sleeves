Coating and Finishing Options

Premium finishes add durability and professional appearance to your food trays. These options provide additional protection against grease, moisture, and wear during food service operations.

Gloss Coating Benefits

Gloss coating creates vibrant color reproduction that makes food photography appear more appetizing and professional. This finish provides moisture resistance that protects printed designs during service periods.

The smooth surface cleans easily and resists staining from food oils and sauces. Colors appear more saturated and professional compared to uncoated tray options.

Matte Coating Advantages

Matte finishes reduce glare under restaurant lighting while providing sophisticated appearance that appeals to upscale customers. This coating option feels smooth but provides better grip for servers and customers.

Fine dining and premium food service operations often choose matte coatings for their elegant positioning. The finish complements contemporary restaurant aesthetics and minimalist design approaches.

Grease-Resistant Coatings

Food service often involves sauces, oils, and moisture that can compromise standard packaging materials. Our grease-resistant coatings prevent penetration and maintain structural integrity throughout service.

This protection keeps printed designs looking professional even with messy food items. The coating also prevents structural weakening that can lead to spills and customer dissatisfaction.
