Custom dessert sleeves are protective wraps designed specifically for handheld desserts and baked goods. They serve multiple business purposes beyond simple mess prevention.

Premium Coating and Finishing Options

Luxury finishes add tactile appeal and professional appearance to your dessert sleeves. These options also provide additional protection against moisture and oils from desserts.

Gloss Coating Benefits

Gloss coating creates vibrant color reproduction that makes dessert photography appear more appetizing. This finish provides moisture resistance that protects printed designs during service.

The smooth, shiny surface feels premium in customers’ hands. Colors appear more saturated and professional compared to uncoated sleeve options.

Matte Coating Advantages

Matte finishes provide sophisticated, elegant appearance that appeals to upscale dessert customers. This coating option feels smooth while providing better grip for handling.

Premium bakeries often choose matte coatings for their understated luxury positioning. The finish complements minimalist designs and contemporary bakery aesthetics perfectly.

Spot UV for Selective Enhancement

Spot UV applies glossy coating to specific design elements while leaving other areas matte. This creates interesting visual contrast that draws attention to logos or key messaging.

The raised texture adds tactile interest that customers notice immediately. This premium option costs more but creates memorable brand experiences that justify higher dessert prices.
