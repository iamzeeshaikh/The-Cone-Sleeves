What are Custom Beverage Sleeves and Why Your Business Needs Them

Custom beverage sleeves are protective wraps designed specifically for hot and cold drink cups. They serve multiple business purposes beyond simple hand protection.

Your brand gains mobile advertising when customers carry drinks around town. Each sleeve becomes a walking advertisement that reaches potential customers throughout the day. This marketing approach costs less than traditional advertising methods.

Food safety regulations often require hand protection for hot beverages. Our sleeves help businesses meet these requirements while adding professional appeal to takeaway orders.

Coating and Finishing Options

Premium finishes add tactile appeal and professional appearance to your beverage sleeves. These options also provide additional protection for artwork during handling.

Gloss Coating Benefits

Gloss coating creates vibrant color reproduction that makes graphics appear more vivid. This finish provides moisture resistance that protects printed designs.

The smooth surface feels premium in customers’ hands. Colors appear more saturated and professional compared to uncoated options.

Matte Coating Advantages

Matte finishes reduce glare and provide sophisticated appearance. This coating option feels smooth but not slippery, improving grip comfort.

Upscale brands often choose matte coatings for their subtle elegance. The finish complements minimalist designs and premium positioning strategies.

Spot UV for Selective Highlighting

Spot UV applies glossy coating to specific design elements while leaving other areas matte. This creates interesting contrast that draws attention to logos or key messaging.

The raised texture adds tactile interest that customers notice immediately. This premium option costs more but creates memorable brand experiences.
