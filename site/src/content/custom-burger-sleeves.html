Coating and Finishing Options for Premium Appeal

Professional finishes add tactile quality and visual appeal to burger sleeve packaging. These options also provide additional protection for printed designs during food handling.

Gloss coating creates vibrant color reproduction that makes brand graphics appear more vivid and professional. This finish provides moisture resistance that protects printed artwork from kitchen humidity.

Matte Coating Advantages

Matte finishes reduce glare while providing sophisticated appearance that appeals to upscale dining concepts. This coating feels smooth without being slippery during handling.

Spot UV applies glossy coating to specific design elements like logos while leaving other areas matte. This creates tactile contrast that customers notice immediately.

The raised texture adds premium feel that justifies higher menu prices. This finishing option costs more but creates memorable brand experiences that encourage repeat visits.
