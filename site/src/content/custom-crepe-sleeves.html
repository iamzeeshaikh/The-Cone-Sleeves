Custom Crepe Sleeves for Food Businesses

We at TCS create custom crepe sleeves that combine style, comfort, and brand identity. If you run a café, street food stall, or dessert shop, you know crepes can be tricky to handle. Fillings can spill, and customers want something they can hold without making a mess. Our sleeves solve that problem while giving you a chance to promote your brand in a simple but effective way.

Made from food-grade paperboard, these sleeves are grease-resistant and comfortable to hold. Each one is custom-printed, turning every crepe into a mini advertisement. Our clients often pair them with other packaging items to create a complete presentation — for example, matching designs on their ice cream cone sleeves to keep branding consistent across all desserts.

Why Use Custom Crepe Sleeves

Crepes are a popular grab-and-go food item, so presentation and convenience matter. Using printed sleeves gives your customers a better experience and makes your brand more memorable.

Better Handling

Sleeves keep hands clean and give a firm grip, which is especially helpful for hot or filled crepes.

Branding on Every Serving

Adding your logo, colors, and taglines on sleeves helps build recognition. Even a simple design looks more professional than a plain paper wrap.
