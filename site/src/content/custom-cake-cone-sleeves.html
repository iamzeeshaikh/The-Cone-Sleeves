What Are Cake Cone Sleeves

Cake cone sleeves are paper wraps that slide over the base portion of a cone. They:

  • Guard fingers from sticky drips

  • Absorb condensation

  • Provide space for branding

  • Help stabilize the cone when held or displayed

They differ from full wrappers in that they cover only the lower part of the cone, leaving the top open for easy access. They work especially well for single-serve cones, dessert cones, or specialty flavored cones.

Design Tips

  • Leave a safe margin (5 mm) so printing doesn’t get cut off

  • Keep critical logos or text within the “print safe zone”

  • Use high contrast so brand elements stand out on cold or moist surfaces

  • Consider spot UV or gloss accents to highlight important visuals
