Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how The Cone Sleeves (“we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and discloses information that we obtain about visitors to our website at https://theconesleeves.com/ (the “Site”) who are customers or potential customers of our Cone Sleeves manufacturing business. By using the Site, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect We collect information about you directly from you and automatically through your use of our Site. The types of information we collect include:

  • Contact information: We collect your name, email address, and other contact information you provide when submitting inquiries or requests through our Site.
  • Usage information: We automatically collect information about your use of our Site, including your IP address, the type of browser you use, the pages you view, and the date and time of your visit.
  • Other information: We may collect additional information you choose to provide through the Site, such as when you participate in surveys or promotions.

Use of Information We may use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your inquiries or requests;
  • To operate and improve our Site and business;
  • To communicate with you about our products and services;
  • To send you promotional materials, newsletters, or other communications that may be of interest to you;
  • To comply with legal obligations; and
  • For any other purpose for which the information was collected.

Disclosure of Information We may disclose the information we collect:

  • To our service providers, who provide services such as website hosting, data analysis, payment processing, and other services on our behalf;
  • To comply with legal obligations, such as responding to a subpoena or court order;
  • To protect our rights, property, or safety, or the rights, property, or safety of others; and
  • With your consent or at your direction.

The Choices You may opt out of receiving promotional emails from us by following the instructions in those emails. If you opt out, we may still send you non-promotional emails, such as those about your account or our ongoing business relations.

Security We take reasonable measures to protect the information we collect from loss, misuse, unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction.

Changes to this Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. The updated Privacy Policy will be posted on our Site, and the updated date will be indicated at the top of the page.

Contact Us If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, don’t hesitate to contact us at info@theconesleeves.com.
